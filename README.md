# Variation-of-Mental-health-illness when comparing to-geographical-location
Variation of Mental health illness when comparing to geographical location
## Question
“How does the frequency of mental health illness and attitudes towards mental health vary by geographic location, and
what are the strongest predictors of mental health illness and specific attitudes towards mental health in the workplace?”
## Data Collection
Sources of data collection were from:
• Kaggle(https://www.kaggle.com/osmi/mental-health-in-tech-survey)
## Analytics
All the studies were further analyzed to find the solution regarding the mental health problems and in answering the questions related to the variation of attitude of mental health in comparison to geographic location.
## Insights
The attitude towards mental health when comparing to the region is as follows
• Positive
• Moderative
• Negative
The work interfere on the comparison of mental health condition when comparing to the region are as follows:
• Never
• Rarely
• Sometimes
• Often
